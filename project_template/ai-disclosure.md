# AI-use disclosure and verification

Complete this file even if you did not use an AI tool materially.

## Assistance used

For each tool, state:

- tool and model, if known;
- task for which it was used;
- which submitted files or passages it influenced; and
- what you changed after reviewing its output.

If there was no material AI assistance, write: “No material AI assistance used.”

## Verification record

For each materially AI-assisted element, record the checks you performed. Relevant evidence may
include direct recalculation of plotted values, inspection of groupings and filters, comparison
with documentation, a fresh top-to-bottom run, or an accessibility check. “The output looked
correct” is not verification.

You remain responsible for every submitted line, chart, and claim.
